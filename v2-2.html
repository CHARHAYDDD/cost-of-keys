Evidence II · Why landlords can be picky

In 2023, fewer than 1 in 70 NYC rentals sat empty.

1.41%
Each dot = 1 in 100 NYC rentals. Red = empty and available.

Lowest since 1968. Under $2,400/mo, vacancy was below 1%. That's why tenants paid the fees: there was always someone else who would. The law took that cost off you.
